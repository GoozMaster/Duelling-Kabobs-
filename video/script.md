# Dueling Kebabs — explainer script

Narrator: male, warm, deadpan cartoon-announcer. Third person, so the story
belongs to the cook without the narrator claiming to be them. Voice rules come
from Springfield Kitchen: short, flat, confident; the app's own words
(pantry, fridge, cuisine, "What can I make?"); no exclamation stacks.

Every number and result on screen is real — pulled from the database and run
through the site's own matcher on 2026-09-26.
